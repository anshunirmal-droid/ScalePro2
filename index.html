The five-pillar growth diagnostic

Diagnose before you optimize.

Before we touch a single campaign, we run a structured diagnostic across five pillars using your Seller Central data, advertising reports, and P&L. Every gap is identified, quantified, and ranked by revenue impact. You leave the audit with a prioritized fix roadmap — whether or not you continue with us.

01. Revenue & Ranking
  • Listing audit
  • Keyword indexing
  • Competitor gap analysis
02. Advertising Optimization
  • TACOS vs. target TACOS
  • Organic vs. sponsored split
  • Harvest-negate cycle setup
03. Profitability & P&L
  • Profit analytics deep dive
  • Margin hunter for packaging
  • Weekly P&L rhythm
04. Inventory & Cash Flow
  • 90-day reorder model
  • Variant demand signals
  • Safety stock framework

  • SKU prioritization and rationalization
  • Geography-2 readiness (UK/CA)
  • Operating rhythm SOPs (weekly & monthly)
How the engagement works

Ninety days from audit to operating rhythm

From the first data pull to a steady weekly cadence — here's exactly what happens, and when.

1
WEEK 1–2

Growth Audit

Deep dive across all five pillars using your Seller Central data, ad reports, and P&L. Every gap identified, quantified, and ranked by revenue impact. Output: a prioritized fix roadmap with week-by-week actions.

2
MONTH 1

Quick Wins

Immediate fixes that recover margin or ranking without waiting for long-term work: fee reconciliation claims filed, negative keywords harvested, listing suppression fixed, reorder triggers set.

3
MONTH 2–3

Structural Fixes

Rebuild what's structurally broken: campaign architecture, inventory model, per-SKU P&L, listing A/B test plan. This is where the compounding work happens.

4
MONTH 4+

Growth Partnership Ongoing

Weekly operating rhythm: ads reviewed Monday, inventory Wednesday, P&L Friday. Monthly business review. Quarterly growth roadmap. Scale plan activated — new SKUs, new geos, new channels.

*Most clients see meaningful movement in TACOS, ranking, or margin within the first 90 days
